CassetteCat for DesktopC-90 · Type II

Side A · Listening

  1. A1
    Your music folderFLAC, MP3, AAC, ALAC, OGG, Opus and WAV, sorted by song, album, artist and folder.
  2. A2
    Your own serverNavidrome, Gonic, Airsonic or Jellyfin, next to your local files.
  3. A3
    Synced lyricsFrom .lrc files, tags or LRCLIB. Tap along once to time plain lyrics.
  4. A4
    EQ with AutoEqTen bands, with correction curves made for your headphones.
  5. A5
    Gapless and crossfadeSongs play back to back, with ReplayGain to keep the volume even.
  6. A6
    Internet radioSearch stations by name, genre or country.

Side B · Your stuff

  1. B1
    Tag editorFix artist, album, genre, year and cover art on many songs at once.
  2. B2
    Listening RecordPlay counts, top artists, your history, and monthly and yearly Rewind.
  3. B3
    ScrobblingLast.fm, ListenBrainz and Libre.fm, plus an optional Discord status.
  4. B4
    Mini playerA small floating player, tray controls and your keyboard's media keys.
  5. B5
    Keyboard shortcutsFor playback, volume, seeking, search and the quick switcher.
  6. B6
    Offline BlackoutOne switch turns off every internet request.
