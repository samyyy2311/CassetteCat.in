FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CassetteCat, privacy, streaming, and open-source licensing.

What is CassetteCat?

CassetteCat is an open-source music player for Android, Windows, and Linux, built for people who want to own their music collection. It plays songs stored on your device, streams from personal Subsonic or Jellyfin libraries, shows synchronized lyrics, and connects to an open-source DIY hardware player.

Does CassetteCat collect or track any user data?

No. There are no analytics libraries, tracking IDs, accounts, or ads. Your listening history and playlists stay strictly on your phone, and server passwords are encrypted locally using the Android Keystore.

What self-hosted servers are supported?

It works with Jellyfin and any server that supports the Subsonic API, such as Navidrome, Gonic, Airsonic, and LMS. You can browse your remote library, stream songs, and cache tracks locally so they play when you are offline.

How do synchronized lyrics work?

CassetteCat first checks for embedded LRC or ID3 tags inside your music files. If they aren't tagged, it pulls live synchronized lyrics from LRCLIB. There is also an in-app lyrics editor if you want to tweak timing or write your own.

What is the CassetteCat DIY hardware player?

It's an open-hardware pocket player powered by an ESP32 chip, with an audio DAC, color display, and SD card storage. You can use the CassetteCat phone app to manage files or control what is playing over local Wi-Fi.

Is CassetteCat compatible with Android Auto?

Yes. It supports Android Auto and Android Automotive OS (AAOS), letting you browse albums, launch playlists, and listen to internet radio right from your car's dashboard.
