CassetteCat for Android

For phones and tablets on Android 8 and later. Get it from Google Play, or download the APK from GitHub and keep it updated with Obtainium. The source code is on GitHub. Every change is reviewed with CodeRabbit.

Everything in the Android app

Playback

How your music sounds and plays.

  • Gapless and crossfadeNo gaps between songs, or a smooth fade.
  • Equalizer with AutoEqFive bands, tuned for your headphones.
  • ReplayGainEven loudness from song to song.
  • Skip SilenceSkips long quiet stretches.
  • Playback speedSlow a song down or speed it up.
  • Volume limitTurns everything down to protect your hearing.
  • Sleep timerStops playback after a set time.
  • Queue and AutoplayResumes where you left off, then plays similar songs.
  • Controls everywhereNotification, lock screen and headphones.
  • Synced lyricsFrom your files or LRCLIB, with an editor.

Library

Your own files, sorted and searchable.

  • Local musicYour folders, in FLAC, MP3, AAC, ALAC, OGG, Opus or WAV.
  • Browse and searchSongs, albums, artists, genres and folders, or a line from the lyrics.
  • PlaylistsYour own and smart playlists, with M3U import.
  • Song detailsEdit tags and covers, and find duplicates.
  • Artist pagesPhotos and biographies, filled in for you.

Streaming and radio

Your server, and stations from around the world.

  • JellyfinYour Jellyfin library, with Quick Connect.
  • Navidrome and SubsonicNavidrome, Gonic, Airsonic, LMS and more.
  • Offline downloadsKeep server songs on your phone.
  • Internet radioThousands of stations from Radio Browser.

Listening Record

What you play, kept on your device.

  • Your statsTop songs, artists and albums, and every listen.
  • RewindA monthly summary to share as a poster.
  • ScrobblingLast.fm, ListenBrainz and Libre.fm.
  • Share cardsShare a song or lyric lines as an image.

Beyond the app

In the car, on the home screen and on your computer.

  • Android AutoYour music on the car's screen, plus Drive Mode.
  • Widget and shortcutsHome screen widget, Quick Settings tile and shortcuts.
  • Listening RoomPlay in sync on several phones. In beta.
  • Your computerControl it and move music between the two.

Yours

Your look, your backups, your privacy.

  • LookAccent colours, pure black mode and fonts.
  • BackupsEverything in one file, restorable anywhere.
  • PrivateNo account, ads or tracking.
