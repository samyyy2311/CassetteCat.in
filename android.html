CassetteCat for AndroidC-90 · Type II

Side A · Listening

  1. A1
    Your local libraryPoint it at your music folders and it reads the tags for you.
  2. A2
    Your own serverNavidrome, Jellyfin, Gonic, Airsonic or LMS. Save songs for when you're offline.
  3. A3
    Synced lyricsRead from your files or pulled from LRCLIB. Fix the timing yourself if it drifts.
  4. A4
    EQ with AutoEqPick your headphones and get a correction curve made for them.
  5. A5
    Internet radioThousands of stations. Search by name, genre or country.
  6. A6
    In the carAndroid Auto, plus a Drive Mode with big, easy buttons.

Side B · Your stuff

  1. B1
    Listening RecordTop songs, artists and albums, every listen, and a monthly Rewind you can share as a poster.
  2. B2
    ScrobblingSend your plays to Last.fm, ListenBrainz or Libre.fm.
  3. B3
    Listening RoomBetaPlay the same song on a few devices across your Wi-Fi.
  4. B4
    Home screen widgetPlay, pause and skip without opening the app.
  5. B5
    BackupsPlaylists, favourites and settings in one file you can restore anywhere.
  6. B6
    Safe loginsServer passwords are encrypted with the Android Keystore.
