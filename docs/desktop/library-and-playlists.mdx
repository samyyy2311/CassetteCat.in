## Music folders

CassetteCat builds your library from the folders you choose, and watches them for new and changed files.

- **Add a folder**: **Settings > Music Library > Audio Folders > Add folder**, or drag a folder from your file manager onto the Library page.
- **Leave a folder out**: add it under **Excluded Folders**, for example a podcasts or voice notes folder inside your music folder.
- **Hide short clips**: **Ignore Short Clips** hides anything shorter than 30 seconds from the library and search.

FLAC, MP3, AAC, M4A, ALAC, OGG, Opus and WAV files all play. CassetteCat reads titles, artists, albums, genres, years, track numbers and cover art from the files' tags, and uses a `cover.jpg` or `folder.jpg` in the album's folder when a file has no cover of its own.

## Browsing

**Library** has tabs for **Songs**, **Artists**, **Albums**, **Genres**, **Folders** and **Playlists**.

- **Refine & Sort** (the sliders button) filters by format (FLAC and lossless, MP3, AAC and M4A) or favourites. It sorts songs by title, artist, album or duration, and artists, albums, genres and folders by name or song count, with albums also by artist.
- **Ctrl+F** (**Cmd+F** on a Mac) searches within the page you're on.
- **The letters down the right edge** of Songs, Artists and Albums jump to that letter when the list is sorted by name. Click or drag along them; the letter you're at is lit.
- **Settings > Music Library > Sorting** sets the default song order. Row spacing and format badges such as FLAC and MP3 are in **Settings > Look & Feel > Track Lists**.

Click an artist or album to open it. Album pages play the album in track order, and artist pages list the artist's albums and songs, with a photo and biography from the online services you allow.

## Search

**Search** (or **Ctrl+K**, **Cmd+K** on a Mac, from anywhere) finds songs, artists and albums as you type. It shows matching artists and albums first, and you can play or shuffle every result at once. Your recent searches are kept on the Search page until you clear them. Format chips limit results to lossless, MP3 or AAC files.

Search also finds songs by their lyrics. Type a line you remember, and songs that have it show under **Lyrics**, with the line that matched. It looks through the lyrics CassetteCat has downloaded or you've added, so to search your whole library, turn on **Find Songs by Their Lyrics** in **Settings > Lyrics**. It downloads lyrics from LRCLIB in the background, one song at a time.

## The song menu

Right-click a song for:

- **Play Next** and **Add to Queue**.
- **Play Next on Phone**, when your phone is paired and playing. See [Phone and computer](/phone-and-computer).
- **Go to Artist** and **Go to Album**.
- **Search Cover Art**, to pick a new cover from online catalogues.
- **Edit Metadata**, to change the song's details. See [Editing tags and covers](#editing-tags-and-covers).
- **Show in Folder**, to open the file in your file manager.
- **Copy Title**.

The heart button on a song, or in the player bar, adds it to your favourites. Favourites appear under **Library > Songs** with the favourites filter, and sync with your phone when it's paired.

## Editing tags and covers

Ctrl-click songs (Cmd-click on a Mac) to select them, then choose **Edit Tags** to change the artist, album, genre, year, track and disc numbers, label, comment and lyrics. With one song selected you can also change its title. With several, a field the songs disagree on shows **Mixed**, and only the fields you edit are changed. **Change cover art** sets one image for all of them. **Find with LRCLIB** fetches lyrics for the song.

**Edit Metadata** in a song's menu edits one song. **Fill from MusicBrainz** there looks the song up on [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org) and fills in its title, artist, album, year, and track and disc numbers. Nothing is saved until you click **Save**.

Changes are written to the files themselves, so other players see them too. A cover you pick with **Search Cover Art** is also saved into the album's files, so File Explorer and other players show it. Turn off **Also save the cover into the music files** under the cover search to keep it in CassetteCat only.

## Library Health

**Settings > Library Health**, or the info button on the Library page, shows how many songs are missing a title, artist, album or cover, and how many are duplicates.

- **Fix Tags** checks every song with MusicBrainz, or only the songs you selected when you start it from **Fix Tags** in the Library's selection bar. It runs in the background at about a song a second, so you can keep listening. If you close the app, **Continue** picks it up where it stopped. When it's done, **Song Details** lists what would change on each song and **Album Covers** lists albums it found a cover for, each with a switch. Nothing is written until you click **Apply**.
- **Duplicates** groups copies of the same song and keeps the best one: lossless first, then the largest file. The copies you pick move to the Recycle Bin (the trash on Linux), so you can restore them.

Fix Tags is careful about which match it uses: the song's length has to agree, an album like "Currents (Deluxe)" keeps its edition, and on a whole-library check a year, track or disc number is only filled in when a song has none. MusicBrainz must be on in **Settings > Network & Services**.

## Playlists

- **New Playlist**: **Library > Playlists > New Playlist**, then drag songs onto it.
- **Smart playlists**: **Favorites**, **Most Played**, **Recently Added** and **Never Played** fill themselves. To make your own, choose **Smart** in **New Playlist** and pick rules, such as added in the last 30 days, favourites only, a song length or a decade. It holds up to 100 songs that match every rule.
- **From a selection**: select songs and choose **Save Playlist**.
- **Save the queue**: **Save Queue** turns the current queue into a playlist.
- **Import and export**: **Import M3U** reads `.m3u` and `.m3u8` playlists from other players, and **Export M3U** writes one out. Songs are matched to files in your library.

Playlists copy between the desktop app and the Android app when the phone is paired.

## Listening Record

**Listening Record** in the sidebar keeps your listening history on this computer, and your phone's too when it's paired. A song counts once you've played most of it; skipping early adds listening time without counting a play.

- **Overview**: listening time, total plays, how much of your library you've explored, your all-time top song, and top artists, songs and albums.
- **Top Tracks**, **Top Artists** and **Top Albums**: full rankings, with play counts.
- **History**: every listen, newest first.
- **Rewind**: one year or one month at a time, with your busiest month, top genre and the songs, artists and albums you played most.

With your phone paired, both apps keep one Listening Record: plays on either device count in the same history and rank the same way. The **Sync** button brings them together straight away.

**Clear listening record** removes the history from this computer, and can't be undone.
