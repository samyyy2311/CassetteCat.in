CassetteCat is translated by the people who use it, on [Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/engage/cassettecat/). You translate in your browser, and Weblate sends your work to the app's repository as a pull request, so you don't need to code or use Git.

<a href="https://hosted.weblate.org/engage/cassettecat/"><img src="https://hosted.weblate.org/widget/cassettecat/multi-auto.svg" alt="How much of CassetteCat each language has translated" /></a>

## What you can translate

| App | Status |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Android** | Every screen is ready to translate. |
| **Desktop** | Being prepared. It isn't on Weblate yet; you can follow [the progress on GitHub](https://github.com/samyyy2311/CassetteCat-Desktop/issues/56). |

Anything not translated yet shows in English, so a language can be useful long before it's finished.

## Start translating

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Weblate">
    Open [CassetteCat on Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/engage/cassettecat/) and sign in, or create a free account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your language">
    Choose your language from the list. If it isn't there, use **Start new translation** to add it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Translate">
    Weblate shows one string at a time, with the English text and any notes. Save each one as you go; you can stop at any point and carry on later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your translations reach the app in its next release. In the desktop app, a new language shows up in Settings once at least 70% of it is translated, so nobody picks a language that's still mostly English.

## Guidelines

- **Write it the way the app would naturally speak** in your language, rather than word for word.
- **Keep placeholders exactly as they are**, such as `%1$s`, `%d` or `%1`. They're filled in with names and numbers.
- **Translate each plural form** Weblate asks for. Languages count differently, and Weblate shows the forms yours needs.
- **Leave names of products and services as they are**, such as CassetteCat, Jellyfin, MusicBrainz or Last.fm.
- **Keep labels short.** Buttons, tabs, widgets, notifications and car screens have little room.

<Tip>
If you can, try your translation in the app once it's released, and look for text that's cut off or reads oddly in context.
</Tip>

## Questions

Ask on Weblate in the string's **Comments**, or [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/samyyy2311/CassetteCat/issues). The [translation guide](https://github.com/samyyy2311/CassetteCat/blob/main/TRANSLATING.md) in the repository has the technical details, such as editing the translation files by hand.
