CassetteCat is a music player for the music you own. It plays songs stored on your device, music on your own Subsonic or Jellyfin server, and internet radio. There are no accounts, ads or tracking.

There are two apps, and each works on its own:

- **CassetteCat for Android**, for Android 8.0 and newer.
- **CassetteCat Desktop**, for Windows, macOS and Linux.

Pair them on the same Wi-Fi and they work as one: your phone becomes a remote, music moves between them, and your likes, playlists and Listening Record stay in sync.
