CassetteCat plays music stored on your phone, music on your own Subsonic or Jellyfin server, and internet radio. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

## Install

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Google Play" icon="store" href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.caffeinelabs.cassettecat">
    Updates arrive through the Play Store.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GitHub" icon="download" href="https://github.com/samyyy2311/CassetteCat/releases/latest">
    Download the APK from the latest release and open it. A `.sha256` file comes with it to check the download.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Obtainium" icon="refresh-cw" href="https://apps.obtainium.imranr.dev/redirect?r=obtainium://add/https://github.com/samyyy2311/CassetteCat">

<Note>
The first time you install the APK yourself, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or file manager. The app can tell you when a new release is out: **Settings > Check for Updates**.
</Note>

## First launch

Setup takes about a minute, and you can change every choice later in **Settings**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome">
    Tap **Set up my library**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Permissions">
    CassetteCat asks for three:

    - **Music and audio**, to find the songs on your phone. Without it, only streaming and radio work.
    - **Notifications**, for playback controls outside the app and on the lock screen.
    - **Nearby devices**, only for pairing a CassetteCat hardware player. You can skip it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your music folders">
    - **All music** finds audio anywhere Android allows. Start here if you're not sure.
    - **Only these folders** builds the library from folders you pick. Handy if your phone also holds podcasts, voice notes or audiobooks you don't want mixed in.
    - **Everything except these folders** scans everything but the folders you pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it yours">
    Pick an accent colour, font, Now Playing backdrop, start screen and gestures. Already have a CassetteCat backup? Tap **Restore from Backup** here to bring back your playlists, favourites and settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your listening device">
    This is for the CassetteCat hardware player. Tap **Skip** if you don't have one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your library appears on **Home** once the scan finishes. If it stays empty, see [Troubleshooting](/android/troubleshooting#library).

## Finding your way around

- **Home**: quick picks, a shuffle button for your whole library, and sections such as Heavy Rotation, Recently Played, Recently Added and Forgotten Favorites.
- **Library**: Songs, Artists, Albums, Genres, Playlists and Folders. You can hide or reorder these tabs in **Settings > Customisation > Navigation > Library Tabs**.
- **Radio**: internet radio stations. See [Streaming, downloads and radio](/android/streaming-and-radio#internet-radio).
- **Search**: searches song titles, artists, albums and lyrics as you type.
- **Settings**: everything else.

You can reorder these tabs, or hide Home, Radio and Search, in **Settings > Customisation > Navigation > Bottom Bar**. Library and Settings always stay.

The **mini-player** at the bottom shows what's playing. Tap it to open **Now Playing**. You can swipe across it to change tracks, and choose the extra button beside play and pause in **Settings > Customisation > Now Playing & Mini-Player > Mini-Player Action**.
