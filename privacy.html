CassetteCat is a free, open-source music player. We don't collect your personal data, we don't track what you listen to, we don't show ads, and your server passwords stay encrypted on your own device.

01.Scope and Summary

This policy covers the CassetteCat Android app (in.caffeinelabs.cassettecat) from Google Play and GitHub Releases, the CassetteCat desktop app for Windows and Linux, and this website at cassettecat.caffeinelabs.in.

CassetteCat plays music stored on your device and streams from servers you run yourself. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no server run by CaffeineLabs that the apps report to.

02.Data We Collect

CaffeineLabs does not receive or store any personal information from CassetteCat.

The apps don't access your name, email address, device identifiers, location, contacts, or microphone. Your playback history, playlists, favourites and listening stats are kept in a local database on your device and are never sent to CaffeineLabs.

If you use an online service, CassetteCat sends only what that request needs. That can be an artist, album or track name for artwork, lyrics, metadata or scrobbling, a search for a radio station, or the login details for a server or scrobbling account you set up yourself.

03.Android Permissions

The Android app asks for these permissions, and uses each one only for the purpose listed:

READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
Finds and plays the music files on your device or SD card. Scanning happens entirely on the device.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS / FOREGROUND_SERVICE
Shows the playback notification and lock screen controls, keeps music playing in the background, and keeps song downloads running.
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Streams from your server and reaches the online services in Section 5.
ACCESS_WIFI_STATE / CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE / CHANGE_NETWORK_STATE / NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES
Used only for the Listening Room and for connecting to a CassetteCat Player. Nearby Wi-Fi access is declared as never used for location.
BLUETOOTH_CONNECT
Shows the name of the connected Bluetooth headphones or speaker on the Now Playing screen. The app does not scan for or pair devices.
DETECT_SCREEN_CAPTURE
On Android 14 and later, offers a share option after you take a screenshot of the player. The app is only told that a screenshot happened. It never sees the image.
VIBRATE
Haptic feedback when you tap controls.

CassetteCat never uploads your music files to CaffeineLabs or to any metadata service. Audio only travels over the network when you stream from a server or radio station you chose, or share it through the local features in Section 6.

04.Passwords and Tokens

When you connect a Subsonic-compatible server (such as Navidrome, Gonic, Airsonic or LMS) or Jellyfin, or sign in to a scrobbling service:

  • Your server address, username and password or token are saved on your device only.
  • On Android, they are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key held in the Android Keystore. The key can't be exported, so it never leaves the device.
  • On desktop, they are stored in your system's password vault: Windows Credential Manager on Windows, and the Secret Service keyring (such as GNOME Keyring or KWallet) on Linux.
  • Login details are only ever sent to the server or service they belong to. HTTPS encrypts them in transit. CassetteCat also allows plain HTTP for your own servers and for a CassetteCat Player on your home network. HTTP connections are not encrypted.

05.Online Services

CassetteCat can use these services for artwork, lyrics, metadata and radio. On Android, each one can be switched off in Settings > External Services, and they are all on by default.

LRCLIB (lrclib.net)
Looks up synced lyrics by song title and artist when a file has no lyrics of its own.
Deezer
Looks up artist photos and album covers by artist and album name.
TheAudioDB
A backup source for artist photos and bios when Deezer has none.
Apple iTunes Search, MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive
Find album covers for songs that don't have one, by album and artist name. These are controlled together by the Cover Art Archive switch. Searching for a cover yourself also uses iTunes Search and Deezer.
Radio Browser
Searches internet radio stations with the words or filters you enter, and plays the station you pick.
Wikipedia and Wikimedia
Fetches short descriptions of artists and albums by name.
GitHub
Checks for new app releases. This has its own switch in Settings > Data & Updates.
ListenBrainz and Libre.fm
Off by default. Only used after you sign in with your own account, and then sends the songs you play to that account.

Each service gets only the request described above and handles it under its own privacy policy. Offline Blackout Mode, available in both the Android and desktop apps, turns off every online request at once, whatever the individual switches say. The desktop app uses the same services, and Offline Blackout Mode is how you switch them off there.

06.Local Network Features

  • Listening Room: when you start a room, your device streams the song that's playing to the other devices in the room over your Wi-Fi, and keeps playback in sync. Nothing goes through the internet or to CaffeineLabs.
  • CassetteCat Player: when you pair the hardware player, the app sends it songs, playback commands and settings over your Wi-Fi or the player's own hotspot, and reads back its battery level, storage and firmware version.

07.Backups

You can save your playlists, favourites, listening stats, folder filters and settings to a backup file. The file is saved wherever you choose, and CaffeineLabs never sees it.

If Android system backup is turned on for your phone, Android may also copy the app's data to your own Google account backup. Server passwords, scrobbling logins and the crash log are always left out of it. Server passwords are never in backups at all, so you'll need to enter them again after a restore.

08.Tracking and Ads

  • No ads: no ad networks, banners or rewarded videos.
  • No analytics: no Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics or similar tools.
  • No tracking: no advertising IDs, fingerprinting or behaviour tracking.

If the Android app crashes, it writes a crash log that stays on your device. You can view it, clear it, or share it yourself from Settings > Privacy & Security if you want to report a bug.

09.This Website

This website has no cookies, analytics or ads. A few things still happen when you visit:

  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, including your IP address, under GitHub's privacy policy.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details.
  • Your browser asks the GitHub API for the star count, download count and latest release links. GitHub receives your IP address for these requests.
  • Those GitHub responses are saved in your browser's local storage for 15 minutes so the page doesn't ask again on every visit. They contain no personal information, and you can clear them by clearing your browser's site data.

10.Deleting Your Data

Everything CassetteCat stores lives on your device, so you are always in control of it. On Android, you can delete it by:

  • Using Settings > Privacy & Security to manage your Listening Record, or to disconnect all servers and remove their saved credentials.
  • Clearing the app's storage in Android settings (Settings > Apps > CassetteCat > Storage > Clear storage).
  • Uninstalling the app.

On desktop, disconnecting a server or scrobbling account deletes its saved password or token from your system's password vault. Uninstalling removes the app itself.

11.Your Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

CaffeineLabs is based in India, and this policy is written to follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The Act gives you the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, and to raise a complaint.

Because CassetteCat doesn't collect or store personal data on any server (see Section 2), these rights are met by design. Your data is on your device, where you can view, change or delete it yourself (see Section 10).

For complaints or data requests, contact us through GitHub Issues (Section 15). If you connect an optional service from Section 5 with your own account, that service's obligations and privacy policy apply to that data.

12.Your Rights Under the GDPR (EEA and UK)

CassetteCat is available worldwide, including in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, where the General Data Protection Regulation applies. It gives you the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or move your personal data, and to object to how it is processed.

CassetteCat processes no personal data on any server, so CaffeineLabs is not a data controller for anything created while you use the apps. That data exists only on your device, under your control, and you can export, change or delete it at any time (see Section 10).

When an optional service from Section 5 handles a request for you, such as a lyrics search sent to LRCLIB, or when you connect your own server (Section 4), that service is an independent data controller under its own privacy policy.

13.Children's Privacy

CassetteCat is not made for children under 13. As Section 2 explains, it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, whatever their age.
